Pricing

The numbers.
Before the sales call.

One Showroom Growth System. A published management fee. Advertising paid directly to the platforms. Month-to-month.

What you pay

  • $1,500 one-time setup: CRM setup, website and search optimization, tracking and ad-account setup within the agreed scope.
  • Monthly management from $1,750: the fee scales with advertising spend, as shown below.
  • Advertising from $1,500 per month: client-funded, paid directly to Google and Meta, separate from management.

Your starting budget

$3,250 per month ongoing at the minimum advertising level: $1,750 management + $1,500 advertising. The first month adds $1,500 setup, for $4,750.

Management fees by ad spend

Monthly advertising spend and corresponding management fees
Monthly advertising spendMonthly management fee
$1,500 minimum$1,750
More than $1,500, up to $3,000$2,500
More than $3,000, up to $5,000$3,500
More than $5,000$3,500 + 10% of the spend above $5,000

For example, $6,000 in monthly advertising means a $3,600 management fee: $3,500 + 10% of $1,000.

What the system includes

  • Local SEO and Google Business Profile optimization.
  • Useful website content and AI-search visibility work.
  • Your own flooring CRM with agreed lead capture and follow-up.
  • Managed Google and Meta advertising. The base scope includes three active Meta ads and one Google campaign.
  • Monthly market adjustments and reporting.

We review your website, current systems, access and connections before agreeing on implementation. Higher advertising budgets and expanded campaigns need an agreed scope.

The 90-day lead guarantee

If we do not deliver at least 15 qualified local leads in your first 90 days, we continue working without an additional management fee until we do. We define a qualified lead with you before launch.

Advertising remains separate and is paid directly to the platforms. This guarantee covers qualified leads, not a particular search ranking, AI recommendation or number of sales.

No long contract

The service is month-to-month. Your specific scope and qualified-lead definition are agreed before starting.

Do I need to replace my website or CRM?

Not automatically. We first review what you already use and what you can change. We can optimize your existing website or rebuild it as part of setup. CRM connections and any transition are agreed before work starts; we do not promise every system will integrate.

Can you guarantee Google rankings or AI recommendations?

No. Search engines and AI services decide which businesses and sources they show. We improve the things we can control: useful pages, accurate business details, technical performance, internal links and clear answers. Our published guarantee covers qualified leads, not a specific ranking or AI mention.

Is the growth audit free?

Yes. The audit is free and there is no obligation to hire us. We review your showroom and discuss what would help before you decide.
