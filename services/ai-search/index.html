AI search visibility

AI search visibility built on useful showroom facts.

Clear, reliable information for the questions homeowners ask search engines and AI tools.

AI search visibility means making your store information easy to find, understand and verify. It complements SEO. No agency can promise that a search engine or AI tool will recommend your showroom.

On this pageWhat we work onWhat your team providesHow we review the workWhat is quoted separately

What we work on

Review buyer questions, strengthen direct answers on relevant pages, align public business details and use structured data that matches visible facts. Check a documented sample of questions and record the answers and sources.

What your team provides

Verified store facts, product and service details, permission to edit relevant pages and listings, real photos and an approver. Claims need evidence before they appear in content.

How we review the work

Compare dated observations for the same questions and tools, recognizing that answers vary. Separate mentions, referrals, inquiries and sold jobs. A small sample is not market-wide visibility or proof that a change caused a result.

What is quoted separately

AI search work is custom quoted. Website production, local SEO campaigns, paid media and optional monitoring tools are specified separately. Google says existing SEO best practices apply to its AI features; there is no special schema requirement.

Source: Google Search Central: AI features and your website. Inclusion and presentation remain Google’s decisions.
