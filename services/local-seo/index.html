Local SEO

Local SEO for flooring stores that need to be found nearby.

Accurate business information and useful local pages for homeowners looking for flooring in your area.

Local SEO connects an accurate Google Business Profile, a useful showroom website and consistent business information. We identify gaps in the area you really serve and agree on the work to address them.

On this pageWhat we work onWhat your team providesHow we review the workWhat is quoted separately

What we work on

Review your Business Profile, categories, services, contact information and key listings. Plan useful service and location content around actual buyer questions, with technical checks and internal links.

What your team provides

Access to the correct profiles and website, accurate hours and service areas, real product and installation details, approved images and content approval. We do not add services you do not offer.

How we review the work

Review completed changes and search visibility alongside relevant calls and forms where tracking is available. A ranking observation is not a sold job; incomplete attribution stays visible.

What is quoted separately

Local SEO is custom quoted. Website changes beyond the agreed scope, paid advertising, media and optional third-party tools are identified separately in the proposal.
