Choosing where to start

How the services fit together

If your website is the bottleneck, start there. When shoppers cannot see what you sell or how to reach you, every other service has less to work with. Local SEO helps people nearby find your store when they search. Ads reach people who are planning a floor right now. AI search work uses the same store facts and answers, made clear enough for AI tools to read and repeat.

Most stores do not need all four at once. We look at your current website and local presence first, then agree which service comes first and what it includes.
