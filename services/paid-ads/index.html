An example to discuss

A carpet-installation ad needs an installation answer.

Someone searching for carpet installation needs a relevant page, a clear service area and a way to discuss the project. Sending that shopper to a general homepage can leave the most useful questions unanswered.

  • The work: agree on the offer and market, match the ad to a relevant page, and review campaign settings and conversion actions.
  • The reviewable output: the approved ad and destination, the separate media budget and management fee, and a definition of the inquiries the campaign is meant to produce.
  • The check: review spend and available inquiry evidence. Separate a phone click or directions tap from an actual conversation, estimate request or sold job.

This is an illustrative campaign decision, not a measured result. Your team handles customer conversations; reporting can connect later outcomes only where the records support it. Landing-page work and creative production belong in the agreed scope.

What we work on

Plan agreed Google or Meta campaigns, match offers to landing pages, review search terms or audience settings and adjust creative and targeting around the available lead-quality evidence.

What your team provides

Ad-account access, an agreed media budget, accurate offers and availability, approved claims and imagery, landing-page access and staff input on inquiry quality. Your team handles customer conversations.

How we review the work

Review spend, inquiry quality and landing-page behavior. When source and sold-job data can be linked, include them with the reporting period and coverage; do not treat platform conversions as verified sales.

What is quoted separately

Ad management is custom quoted. Media is paid separately to the platforms. Campaign scope, creative production, landing-page work and optional tools are defined before commitment.

Start with the service you need

A new CDL website is not required. We can use your existing site when its access, compatibility and inquiry paths support the agreed work. Any necessary changes are scoped first. This service has its own fee; advertising spend is separate.
