Paid ads

Flooring store advertising with a clear next step.

Managed Google and Meta ads built around the people you serve, the products you sell and the work your showroom can take on.

Flooring store advertising should connect a relevant homeowner with a useful page and an easy way to ask for help. We manage campaign targeting, copy, creative and adjustments across Google and Meta, while your advertising budget is paid directly to the platforms.

Meet demand on Google.

Search campaigns focus on the services and product categories relevant to your showroom and service area. We connect ads to the matching page, review search terms and adjust around lead quality.

Build interest on Facebook and Instagram.

Meta campaigns introduce your showroom and offers to a local audience. Creative should reflect your real products and approved claims. Retargeting is considered where your tracking setup and audience make it appropriate.

Keep the offer and destination connected.

An ad for carpet should land on a page about carpet. A homeowner should understand the offer, what happens next and how to reach the showroom without searching the rest of the site.

Separate advertising spend from management.

The base scope includes three active Meta ads and one Google campaign. Minimum advertising is $1,500 per month, paid directly to the platforms. Management starts at $1,750 per month and scales with spend. We review scope before adding campaigns.

Part of the Showroom Growth System

Local SEO, CRM, managed ads and monthly adjustments. Setup is $1,500 once. Management starts at $1,750 per month, plus at least $1,500 in monthly advertising paid directly to platforms.

Questions about paid ads

Is ad spend included in your fee?

No. Advertising is paid directly to the platforms. Management covers running the agreed campaigns alongside the other services in the growth system.

Do ads guarantee sales?

No. Product fit, pricing, showroom capacity and follow-up affect what happens after an inquiry. We evaluate lead quality and sold jobs where the data supports it.
