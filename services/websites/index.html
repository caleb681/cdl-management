Showroom websites

Flooring showroom websites that help people take the next step.

Recommended standalone website plan: $500/month + $1,500 one-time setup. A useful website for one showroom, with hosting, care and monthly updates.

A flooring showroom website should quickly answer three questions: does this store have what I need, does it serve my area and how do I get help? We organize the agreed pages around those decisions.

On this pageWhat we work onWhat your team providesHow we review the workWhat is quoted separately

What we work on

One showroom. Up to 8 content pages, mobile design, clear copy, search foundations, hosting and care. Includes 45 minutes of updates each month. Setup includes one brand direction, up to 5,000 words, 20 supplied images, two consolidated revision rounds and up to two pages formatting your supplied legal text. One standard inquiry form, search foundations, measurement setup and up to 20 agreed redirects are included. See the full build scope.

What your team provides

Your store details, product and installation facts, approved photography, brand requirements, website access and a person who can review the content. We assess co-op and provider restrictions first.

How we review the work

You review the design/content draft and the final version before launch. We check mobile/desktop pages, keyboard use, links, redirects and real inquiry delivery to the agreed destination, with your authorization. Monthly care includes availability monitoring, link/form checks, versioned backups, restoration and a short care summary.

What is quoted separately

$500/month + $1,500 one-time setup. Local SEO campaigns, ongoing AI-search work, advertising management and licensed product tools are quoted separately. Ad spend is extra. CRM integration is planned for later and is not included. New pages, redesigns, integrations and bulk imports need a separate scope. Extra work is $125/hour or a written fixed quote, approved before work begins. Month-to-month after launch. Cancel with 30 days’ notice, with a prorated final month. Your domain and accounts stay yours; a portable website handover is included after setup is paid.
