Flooring CRM

A flooring CRM that keeps the next step in view.

Bring inquiries, conversations and unsold quotes into a clearer follow-up process, with a setup agreed around your showroom.

A flooring CRM organizes the work between a new inquiry and a sold job. It gives your team a place to see leads, conversations and follow-up tasks. Our client offering includes lead capture and configured follow-up, with the details checked before launch.

Give the team a shared picture.

A new form, call or ad inquiry should have a clear next action. We agree on how leads enter your pipeline and what your team needs to see. Capture depends on the connections actually configured for your business.

Keep busy weeks from ending the conversation.

Configured email, text follow-up and missed-call text-back can support your team. Timing, sender setup, permissions and opt-out handling need to be in place before those features are activated.

Make unsold quotes easier to follow up.

A quote sent is not a job sold. The pipeline should help staff see what has been discussed, what a customer needs next and when to follow up, without treating every unanswered inquiry as a sale.

Agree on the change before making it.

We review your current tools, available integrations and data first. This client CRM offer is not a promise to replace your flooring business software, estimating system, inventory or accounting. Import and connection requirements are confirmed in scope.
