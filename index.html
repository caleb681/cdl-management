Questions

Everything you're probably wondering

Do I have to sign a long contract? +

No. Everything is month-to-month. We'd rather earn your business every 30 days by producing results than lock you into something you can't leave.

Is the ad spend included in the monthly price? +

No, and that's on purpose. Your ad budget is paid straight to Google and Facebook so every dollar goes to reaching customers, not to us. Your management fee covers building, running, and optimizing those campaigns plus your SEO, GEO, and CRM.

What does the CRM actually do? +

It's your own CRM that captures every lead from your site and ads, texts and emails them back automatically (including missed-call text-back), and keeps following up until they book, so busy weeks don't cost you sales.

How does the 90-day guarantee work? +

If we don't generate at least 15 qualified local leads in your first 90 days, we keep working at no additional management cost until we do. We define "qualified" together up front so it's crystal clear.

What if I already have a website? +

Great. We'll optimize what you have or rebuild it as part of setup, whichever gets you customers faster. Either way it's covered in the one-time setup fee.

How soon will I see results? +

Paid ads and CRM follow-up can start producing leads within the first couple of weeks. SEO and GEO compound over the following months, which is exactly why the guarantee spans a full 90 days.
