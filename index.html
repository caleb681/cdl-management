Ask us something else →
Who does CDL Marketing work with?

Our main focus is independent flooring showrooms. We also have experience operating local businesses in restoration, assisted living, fitness and massage. If you run a flooring showroom, this site is built around the problems you face every day.

What services are included?

The Showroom Growth System combines local SEO, Google Business Profile work, AI-search content, your own flooring CRM, managed Google and Meta ads, and monthly market adjustments. The base advertising scope is three active Meta ads and one Google campaign. See the complete pricing and scope.

How is CDL different from a general agency?

We run flooring showrooms ourselves, including Kirksville Carpet One, Springfield Carpet One and Columbia Rug Carpet One in Peoria. That gives us direct experience with product questions, estimates, unsold quotes and the work between an inquiry and a sold job.

How long does flooring marketing take to work?

Ads and follow-up can reach buyers sooner than organic search work, but timing depends on your market, starting point and offer. SEO usually needs sustained work over months. We agree on what a qualified lead means before starting and review the first 90 days against that definition.

Do I need to replace my website or CRM?

Not automatically. We first review what you already use and what you can change. We can optimize your existing website or rebuild it as part of setup. CRM connections and any transition are agreed before work starts; we do not promise every system will integrate.

How much does it cost?

Setup is $1,500 once. Monthly management starts at $1,750, with at least $1,500 per month in advertising paid directly to the platforms. That is a starting ongoing total of $3,250 per month, plus setup in the first month. Management scales with ad spend. See the full fee table.

Can you guarantee Google rankings or AI recommendations?

No. Search engines and AI services decide which businesses and sources they show. We improve the things we can control: useful pages, accurate business details, technical performance, internal links and clear answers. Our published guarantee covers qualified leads, not a specific ranking or AI mention.

How does the 90-day lead guarantee work?

If we do not deliver 15 qualified local leads in your first 90 days, we continue working without an additional management fee until we do. We define a qualified lead with you before launch. Advertising is paid directly to the platforms and remains separate. Read the guarantee.
