Ask us something else →
Who is CDL Marketing a good fit for?

Independent flooring showroom owners who want practical help with their website, local visibility or advertising. We first check your goals, market, current tools and access before recommending a scope.

What do you offer?

Showroom websites, local SEO, Google and Meta advertising, and AI search visibility. We agree on the work you need rather than assuming every store needs every service. Explore the services.

How is CDL different from a general agency?

We operate Kirksville Carpet One, Springfield Carpet One and Columbia Rug Carpet One in Peoria. That gives us direct experience with product questions, estimates and the work behind a sold job. See our operating background.

How long does flooring marketing take?

Timing depends on your market, starting point, access and agreed work. Ads can reach buyers while a campaign runs; organic visibility needs sustained work. The first 90 days are a planning and review period, not a promised sales result.

Do I need to replace my website or CRM?

A new CDL website is not required. We can scope SEO or advertising around your existing site when access, compatibility and inquiry paths support the work. We assess those first. CRM integration is planned for later and is not part of our current offer. Read our approach to existing systems.

What does no long-term marketing contract mean?

Month-to-month SEO and advertising for independent flooring showrooms across the United States. We agree the work, fees and cancellation notice in writing before starting. Month-to-month does not remove the need for an agreement or cancel your current provider’s contract. Website-only plan terms and any approved switching credits are recorded separately. Review the proposed switching support.

What does the website plan include?

Our proposed standalone website plan for review is $500/month + $1,500 one-time setup. One showroom. Up to 8 content pages, mobile design, clear copy, search foundations, hosting and care. Includes 45 minutes of updates each month. Setup includes 2 consolidated revision rounds. Month-to-month after launch. Cancel with 30 days’ notice, with a prorated final month. Your domain and accounts stay yours; a portable website handover is included after setup is paid. Local SEO campaigns, ongoing AI-search work, advertising management and licensed product tools are quoted separately. Ad spend is extra. CRM integration is planned for later and is not included. See the full scope and terms.

Can you guarantee rankings, AI mentions or leads?

No. We do not promise a search position, AI recommendation, lead count or sales result. We agree on deliverables and review the evidence, including gaps in tracking.

What will I receive in the free audit?

A review of your website and local online presence, with three priority opportunities to discuss. It is a practical starting point, with no obligation to hire us.
