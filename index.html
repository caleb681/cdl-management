Our approach is different

No long contract.
Just the work.

For too long, flooring stores have signed marketing contracts that promised a lot and never delivered. Our approach is different. If we don’t do what we say, you can let us go and move on. No waiting for a contract to end. We fight every day to keep our clients.

We’ve seen stores pay for a 12-month SEO contract and get 12 edits in 12 months. That isn’t SEO. Search, your Google listing, your website and your ads all need to line up, and they have to be tuned all the time.

Think about your best installer

The little things.
Every time.

A good installer does the floor prep, patches, uses the right adhesive, vacuums and talks to the customer. That’s how they get five-star reviews. Marketing works the same way. Google changes every couple of weeks, so we check the work a few days later, not a year later, and adjust.

Your installer is the last thing a customer sees. We take care of the first: Google, Maps and what ChatGPT recommends. Nothing magical. We’re just doing the work.

We’re in the flooring business, too

From a product question
to a measure appointment.

A flooring inquiry is the start of a real project. The shopper may need help comparing materials, understanding installation or arranging a measure. Our team operates flooring showrooms and handles marketing for our own businesses, so those decisions shape the pages and campaigns we plan.

What does a flooring marketing agency do?

A flooring marketing agency helps flooring stores and flooring companies get found and get more estimate requests: SEO, your Google Business Profile, your website, Google and Facebook ads and, now, AI search. All of these need to line up, and they have to be tuned all the time. CDL Marketing does that work for flooring stores, and we run our own flooring showrooms too.

Who is CDL Marketing a good fit for?

Independent flooring showroom owners who want practical help with their website, local visibility or advertising. We first check your goals, market, current tools and access before recommending a scope.

What do you offer?

Showroom websites, local SEO, Google and Meta advertising, and AI search visibility. We agree on the work you need rather than assuming every store needs every service. Explore the services.

How is CDL different from a general agency?

Our flooring-industry experience includes product questions, estimates and the work behind a sold job. See our operating background.

How long does flooring marketing take?

Timing depends on your market, starting point, access and agreed work. Ads can reach buyers while a campaign runs; organic visibility needs sustained work. The first 90 days are a planning and review period, not a promised sales result.

Do I need a new website?

No. A new CDL website is not required. We can do SEO or advertising around your existing site when access and inquiry paths support the work. We assess those first, and we will point out free fixes on your current site whether or not you hire us.

What does no long-term marketing contract mean?

Month-to-month SEO and advertising for independent flooring showrooms across the United States. We agree the work, fees and cancellation notice in writing before starting. Month-to-month does not remove the need for an agreement or cancel your current provider’s contract. Website-only plan terms and any approved switching credits are recorded separately. Review the proposed switching support.

What does the website plan include?

Our proposed standalone website plan for review is $500/month + $1,500 one-time setup. One showroom. Up to 8 content pages, mobile design, clear copy, search foundations, hosting and care. Includes 45 minutes of updates each month. Setup includes 2 consolidated revision rounds. Month-to-month after launch. Cancel with 30 days’ notice, with a prorated final month. Your domain and accounts stay yours; a portable website handover is included after setup is paid. Local SEO campaigns, ongoing AI-search work, advertising management and licensed product tools are quoted separately. Ad spend is extra. See the full scope and terms.

How do flooring stores show up in ChatGPT and AI search?

AI tools pull from your website, your Google Business Profile, your reviews and other sites that mention you. Clear, consistent store facts and direct answers to real buyer questions make you easier to find and quote. See our AI search work.

Can you guarantee rankings, AI mentions or leads?

No. We do not promise a search position, AI recommendation, lead count or sales result. We agree on deliverables and review the evidence, including gaps in tracking.

What will I receive in the free audit?

A review of your website and local online presence, with three priority opportunities to discuss. It is a practical starting point, with no obligation to hire us.
