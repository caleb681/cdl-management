Reviewed November 5, 2026 · Published by CDL Marketing

Considering a Broadlume alternative? Compare the work your showroom needs, the systems you already use and the terms in each proposal. Broadlume, now a Cyncly company, offers a broad flooring technology platform. CDL brings an operator's perspective to a focused marketing engagement.

Where Broadlume and Cyncly are strong

Cyncly's flooring website packages list product catalogs, sample ordering, visualization, reporting, call tracking and reputation tools. Higher tiers add Retail Lead Management seats, an account manager and additional marketing services. Its current package table explains those distinctions.

Broadlume also advertises flooring-specific SEO, including content, local listings and backlinks. Cyncly's October 2026 guidance on AI and showroom shopping discusses AI answer engines and connecting online exploration to customer inquiries. These are real parts of its published offer and positioning.

What CDL brings to the decision

We run flooring showrooms ourselves. That experience shapes how we approach a homeowner's search, product questions and first contact with the store. Meet the people behind CDL and the businesses that inform our work.

Our current offer centers on local SEO, paid advertising, showroom websites and AI search visibility. We agree on the work before starting. CRM integration is planned for later and is not available in our current offer.

No long-term marketing contract. Month-to-month SEO and advertising for independent flooring showrooms across the United States. We agree the work, fees and cancellation notice in writing before starting.

Choose the fit before comparing the fee

If an integrated catalog, visualizer or retail software package is central to your decision, review those tools in detail. If you want a focused marketing scope around your existing site and systems where access allows, start with the work CDL would take on. Request equivalent deliverables and separate costs before comparing proposals.
