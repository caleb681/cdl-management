What assisted living marketing is

Assisted living marketing is the work of helping a family find your home, trust it and book a tour. For an independent home it comes down to five things: being found when someone searches for care in your town, a website that answers their questions, reviews they can believe, steady relationships with the people who refer families, and a fast, kind answer when they call.

It is not a billboard budget or a clever slogan. The family looking is usually an adult daughter or son, on a phone, soon after a fall, a hospital stay or a diagnosis. They are comparing three to five homes and they want straight answers. The home that gives them wins more often than the home that spends more.

Where move-ins come from

Most small homes fill rooms from four places: professionals who refer families (hospital discharge planners, home health and hospice agencies, elder law attorneys, physicians), word of mouth, families who find the home online and call directly, and paid referral directories. Referral directories are a real source for many homes, and they are commonly paid a fee per move-in set by your contract. Every move-in you earn directly is one you do not pay that fee on. Our work is aimed at that direct share.

Where we start

Every home is different, but the order rarely changes. We fix what loses families before we pay to reach more of them.

  1. The inquiry path. We call your number and send your web form the way a family would, including after hours. If an inquiry can go unanswered, that comes first.
  2. Your Google listing. Name, address, phone, hours, category, real photos and a plain description of the care you provide.
  3. Your website. Care levels, cost, how to tour and a phone number that can be tapped, on a page that loads quickly on a phone.
  4. Reviews and listings. A steady, honest way to ask every family, and corrected details on the large directories.
  5. Ads, if rooms are still open. A small Google search campaign aimed at families looking in your town, judged by tours and move-ins.

Common questions

What does an assisted living marketing agency do?

It helps families find your home and take the next step. For us that means your website, your Google listing and reviews, your listings on other sites, and Google or Facebook ads when they make sense. We also help you track where each inquiry and move-in came from.

Who do you work with?

Independent assisted living and memory care homes, usually one to three locations. We do not work with national chains. Our work is built for an owner or administrator who does the marketing on top of everything else.

Is there a long contract?

No. The work is month to month. You own your website, your domain, your Google listing and your ad accounts, and you keep them if you leave.

Do you promise a number of move-ins?

No. Nobody can honestly promise move-ins or a search position, and we will not. We tell you what we will do each month, show you what changed, and count inquiries, tours and move-ins by source with you.

Will you use photos or stories of our residents?

Only with written consent that you have on file, and from the legal representative when a resident cannot give it. When there is no consent we use photos of the building, rooms, meals and grounds.
