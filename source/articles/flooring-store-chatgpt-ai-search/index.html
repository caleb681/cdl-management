How flooring stores can show up in ChatGPT and AI search

By Caleb Foglesong · October 30, 2026

Updated November 4, 2026. Original publication: October 30, 2026.

A flooring store can make its public information easier to find and verify: what it sells, where it is and how customers can reach it. No agency can guarantee an organic AI recommendation. We run three Carpet One showrooms in Kirksville and Springfield, Missouri, and Peoria, Illinois. Below is a suggested check, alongside work recorded at our stores in October 2026.

Here is a repeatable check you can try, the October 2026 work behind our examples, and the limits of what those examples prove.

What happens when someone asks an AI tool for a flooring store

A shopper may ask an AI search service "best flooring store in Peoria IL." Depending on the service and mode, it may search the web, summarize information and link to sources. A generated answer and ordinary search results can both be part of the experience.

Keep your store facts clear for anyone checking them: what you sell, where you are, the area you serve and how to get help. These details help shoppers. They are not a formula that guarantees an AI service will name your store.

These answers also change. The same question can get a different answer from one day to the next, and from one tool to another. One answer is a limited observation. Repeated checks can reveal patterns, but cannot prove that a website change caused an AI recommendation.

A repeatable check you can try

Choose a consistent set of homeowner questions about your market, such as "best flooring store in Peoria IL." Compare answers across relevant search services. This is a suggested method, not a report of a completed monthly experiment or measured results from our stores.

  1. Write your questions. Start with a manageable set. Mix the types:
    • "Best flooring store in [town]"
    • "Where can I buy luxury vinyl plank near [town]"
    • "Who installs carpet in [town]"
    • "Hardwood flooring store near [town]"
    • "Cabinet store in [town]," if you sell cabinets
  2. Record the conditions. Save the exact question, service, model or search mode where shown, date, time and search location. Use a fresh session and record whether you were signed in; that does not eliminate every source of personalization.
  3. Score consistently. Record whether your store is named and whether its own website is cited. Save the source URLs, incorrect details and other businesses named. A brand mention and a website citation are different observations.
  4. Save the evidence. Keep the full answer or screenshot with those conditions so it can be checked later.
  5. Look for useful corrections. Check inaccurate or missing facts against your website and business profiles. A competitor being named does not prove which factor caused the choice.
  6. Repeat and compare. A monthly review can be a practical starting schedule. Keep the questions and conditions comparable, note any differences and look at trends across repeated observations.

What to fix first

The following changes were recorded at our own stores in October 2026. They are examples of work performed, not proof of an AI visibility increase. They are the same basics that help regular local search, which is part of the point. You are not building a second marketing plan for AI. You are making the facts about your store easy to find and easy to trust.

Your own website, under your control

On October 26, 2026, we moved our three stores off the corporate co-op websites and onto one website per store that we control. That gave us control over page content and technical changes. A co-op website may still be workable; the plan depends on the access and permissions available.

Answer-first guide pages

In October 2026 we added a Flooring Guides section to each store site. The articles explain real buying questions and local service details. Some included FAQ markup, which should not be treated as a ranking or citation benefit. Our Springfield store's vinyl plank installation guide is one example.

Write an opening that helps the reader understand the answer. Google requires no two-sentence format or special AI schema. FAQ rich results ended May 7, 2026; useful questions and answers can remain.

Schema on your homepage

In October 2026 we added schema to every store homepage. It states the store's name, address, phone and hours in a format machines read directly.

Matching listings everywhere

Our October 2026 record described matching store details on Bing Places, Facebook, BBB and Houzz. Bing Places was recorded complete for all three stores; the other listings were still under review. Some AI tools draw on search engines other than Google, so claim and fix your Bing Places listing, not just your Google profile.

A complete Business Profile

Our October 2026 work across nine Business Profiles included descriptions, services (our Peoria store went from 9 listed services to 22), https links and holiday hours. The checklist also called for weekly posts and review replies; that is not proof that every later post or reply happened. Details are in Google Business Profile for flooring stores: the setup we use.

Reviews, with replies

Reviews are one of the few places where customers describe your store in their own words. Ask for them after every job and reply to every one.

What not to do

  • Don't claim "best" on your own site. Write facts that can be checked instead: what you sell, where you are, how your pricing works.
  • Don't stuff pages with town names. One clear page per real question beats ten pages that swap one town for another.
  • Don't buy or fake reviews.
  • Don't panic over one bad answer. Look at the trend over a few months.

What we don't know yet

We are not going to tell you AI search is replacing Google. We don't know that, and neither does anyone selling it as a sure thing. We also have not yet measured sold jobs from AI answers at our stores. Our scoreboard is sold jobs, and for AI search, that part is still unmeasured.

What we do know is that some shoppers are already asking these tools where to buy floors, a check takes staff time and depends on the tools available, and accurate business facts remain useful to customers. FAQ

How do I get my flooring store to show up in ChatGPT?

Keep your website and business information accurate. For ChatGPT search eligibility, allow OAI-SearchBot and its published IPs through your host or CDN. Placement is not guaranteed. Search and training crawlers have separate controls. See OpenAI's crawler documentation and search guidance.

How often should I check what AI search says about my store?

A monthly review can be a practical starting point, not a required or proven frequency. Save the question, model or mode, date, location, full answer and citation URLs. Compare repeated observations and label changes in conditions.

Is AI search optimization different from regular SEO for a flooring store?

For Google, ordinary SEO foundations still apply. No particular answer length or special AI markup guarantees inclusion. Other services have their own crawler controls.

Which AI tools should a flooring store check?

Possible services include ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI search features. Choose those relevant to your customers, identify the search mode and keep the method consistent. This list is not a claim that we completed checks on each service.
