About CDL

We know what it is like
to run a showroom.

CDL Marketing is part of CDL Management, based in Kirksville, Missouri. Caleb Foglesong and our team operate local businesses and do the marketing for them, too.

Our perspective

Start with the work
behind the sale.

Marketing is only part of the job. Someone has to answer the inquiry, help the homeowner, arrange the estimate and follow up on the quote. That is the world we work in.

Our flooring experience includes Kirksville Carpet One, Springfield Carpet One and Columbia Rug Carpet One in Peoria, along with ProSource wholesale showrooms. Our broader operating experience also includes other local service businesses.

We bring that practical perspective to local search, ads, websites and follow-up for independent flooring showrooms.
