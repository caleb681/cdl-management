About CDL

We know what it is like
to run a showroom.

CDL Marketing is part of CDL Management, based in Kirksville, Missouri. Caleb Foglesong and our team operate local businesses and do the marketing for them, too.

Our perspective

Start with the work
behind the sale.

Marketing is only part of the job. Someone has to answer the inquiry, help the homeowner, arrange the estimate and follow up on the quote. That is the world we work in.

Our background includes flooring retail, wholesale showrooms and other local service businesses.

We bring that practical perspective to websites, local search, advertising and AI search visibility for independent flooring showrooms.

A new website is not required to discuss SEO or advertising. We first review access, compatibility and the inquiry path. Keep useful pages and tools where they support the agreed work.
