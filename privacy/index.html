Privacy policy

What we collect through this website, why, and what we do with it.

Last updated November 5, 2026

CDL Marketing ("we", "us") runs cdlmanagement.com. This policy describes information processed through this website and how to contact us about it.

Information you send us

We collect the contact and business details you choose to send through our forms.

  • Growth audit requests: your name, showroom name, city and state, email, and any optional phone number, website, revenue range or goals you provide.
  • Homepage design requests: your contact and business details, business type and any optional information you enter.

We use these details to review your request, prepare an audit or design, respond to you and keep a record of the conversation. Submitting a request does not require allowing advertising measurement.

Hosting and abuse protection

Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, processes technical information such as network addresses, browser information and requested pages to deliver and protect the site. Our request system also uses short-lived keyed identifiers derived from network and request information to limit abuse and duplicate submissions. These protective checks are separate from optional advertising measurement.

Who handles requests

Growth audit and homepage design forms send requests to our website's intake service for our team to review. Hosting, intake and advertising providers process information through their services under the applicable arrangements and their own policies. We may also disclose information where required by law.

We use the details you submit to respond to your request. Advertising measurement involves a separate provider and choice, as described below.

Optional advertising measurement

Advertising measurement is off until you allow it in Privacy choices. If you allow it, this site requests the Meta Pixel script and a page-view event. After a form request is acknowledged, our site code can request a lead event identifying the form type. Our site code does not add your entered contact fields to that event. This describes our site code, not every operation of Meta's script or account-configured tools. Meta may process browser, device and activity information through its services; see Meta's Privacy Policy.

Google Analytics is not currently configured on this website. Browser settings and blocking tools can affect whether permitted measurement loads or sends events.

Your measurement choice

You can allow or decline optional advertising measurement using Privacy choices in the footer. We store your choice and time in this browser for up to 180 days. Clearing browser storage removes that saved choice. Where detected, Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track signals keep this measurement off, even if an earlier choice allowed it. Browser support varies; an absent signal does not count as permission.

Declining stops our site code from requesting new measurement events. If the Meta script was already requested, it may remain active on that page. Once the off choice can be applied, the controls offer an explicit reload to use a fresh page. Copy or finish any form details before reloading. Changing a choice cannot retract information already sent or delete information held by a provider. If your browser cannot save a choice, measurement stays off on the current page and the controls explain that limitation.

Contact, retention and requests

If you provide contact details, we may contact you about your request. To ask us to stop contacting you, or to request access to, correction of or deletion of information you submitted, email caleb@cdl1.net. We review requests against the information and records we hold and any applicable obligations.

Contact and business records may be kept while needed to handle requests, provide agreed services and maintain business records. This policy does not set a fixed deletion period for accepted submissions. The short-lived abuse-protection identifiers are separate from those records. Contact us with a retention question about your information.

Children

This website is intended for business owners. We do not knowingly seek information from children.

Changes and questions

We update the date above when this policy changes. For questions about this website's information handling, email caleb@cdl1.net or call (660) 665-3889.
