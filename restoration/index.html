Why CDL

We have paid for
these clicks ourselves.

CDL Management, the company behind CDL Marketing, also owns and runs a disaster restoration company that handles water, fire and mold jobs. We run its Google Ads, its Local Services Ads, its website and its Google listing, and we judge all of it on booked jobs.

That means the advice on this site is not theory. Some of what we found in our own accounts:

  • The lead is a phone call. Over one 30-day stretch, every lead our Google accounts recorded was a call. In one market, 5 came from search ads and 12 from Local Services Ads.
  • Water clicks are expensive, even in a small town. Google’s first-page price on our water damage searches ran from $15.53 to $59.43 a click.
  • Facebook did not sell water damage. We spent $1,412 on two campaigns and got zero form leads. It did sell mold inspections and cleaning jobs.
  • Money leaks quietly. In one campaign, about 83% of the spend was going to people who searched our own company name.

How we work and what to expect.

Ask us something else →
What is restoration marketing?

Restoration marketing is the work that gets a water, fire or mold company found and called: your Google Business Profile, your website, Google Ads, Local Services Ads, reviews and now AI search. Most water jobs start with a phone search in an emergency, so the work is about being found fast and answering the call.

Who do you work with?

Independent restoration companies in the United States that do water, fire and mold work with their own crew. We are built for the owner who is not part of a national franchise and has no corporate marketing department behind them.

Do you sell leads?

No. We do not sell or resell leads. We build and run marketing in your own Google accounts, so the calls come to your number and go to nobody else. If you stop working with us, the accounts, the listing and the website stay yours.

Is there a contract?

Marketing is month to month. We agree the work, the fee and the notice period in writing before anything starts. There is no 12-month term.

What does it cost?

It depends on what you need, so we quote in writing after the free audit. Your ad budget is separate and is paid straight to Google. Water damage clicks are expensive almost everywhere, so we talk through a realistic budget for your market before you spend anything.

Do you guarantee leads or rankings?

No. Nobody controls Google, the weather or how many pipes burst in your town. We promise the work, show it to you every month, and compare it with the calls and jobs you can verify.

How long does it take?

Ads and Local Services Ads can bring calls within days of going live. Google listing and website changes usually take weeks to show. Ranking for the main phrases in a competitive city takes months.
