What running a home taught us

Four things we hold to.

Small is a strength. Say so.

A small home cannot outspend a chain and does not need to. Staff who know every resident is something a family can see on a tour. We build the marketing around what is true about your home.

Families want straight answers on cost.

The home that explains its prices clearly earns trust first. We will push you to be clear about cost on your website.

No pressure. Ever.

This is one of the hardest decisions a family makes. We do not write countdowns, scare tactics or promises about health outcomes, for you or for us.

Residents come before content.

No resident photo or story is used without written consent on file. When there is no consent, we show the building, the rooms, the meals and the grounds.

How we judge the work

Tours and move-ins, by source.

Rankings and clicks are not the finish line. We count inquiries, tours and move-ins by where they came from, and we say so when the tracking is incomplete. We do not guarantee move-ins or search positions. Nobody honestly can.

Who will I work with?

Caleb is your first contact. Before any paid work starts, we agree in writing on what we will do each month, who approves it and how progress is reviewed. The work is month to month, and your website, domain, Google listing and ad accounts stay yours.
