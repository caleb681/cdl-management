Start with the actual agreement

A clear move starts with a review.

The review can cover Broadlume or another website provider. We need to understand what you have, what you can move and what you still owe. We do not assume every provider uses the same contract or that every website, catalog or tool can be transferred.

  • Your current provider, services and known contract end date.
  • The applicable agreement, renewal and cancellation requirements, with private information redacted if documents are later requested.
  • Who controls your domain, hosting, content and accounts, and which tools or licenses need replacing.
  • The important URLs, inquiry destinations and tracking that must survive the move.

No file upload is required to ask about switching. Start with a conversation about your current website. Review is not approval of a credit or a legal interpretation of your agreement.

A controlled handover

Four steps to switching.

  1. Review the existing agreement.

    Confirm the provider, end date, notice, access and transfer rights before proposing a date. Keep the current website and inquiry paths working.

  2. Approve the scope and credits.

    Agree the website, any paid marketing, separate media budget and exact eligibility, credit period, setup fee and cancellation terms in writing. No credit is granted just by making an inquiry.

  3. Build and verify.

    Prepare the new site, inventory important URLs, review redirects and test pages, inquiry delivery and agreed tracking. Get approval of the exact build.

  4. Control the cutover.

    Agree who changes what and when, verify the new site and contact paths, and retain a rollback plan. You handle existing-provider cancellation under your agreement. We do not cancel accounts or move domains without authorization.

Proposed, with terms still open

What we must agree before offering a credit.

Proposed monthly website-service credits; setup and marketing/ad spend quoted separately. No credit is automatically granted. Eligibility, dates and limits need review and written approval before work begins.

  • Eligibility and current agreement: Which existing providers and agreements qualify, what evidence is needed, and the actual end date, renewal and cancellation requirements. Broadlume and other providers can be reviewed; no provider’s customers automatically qualify.
  • Paid marketing and advertising budget: The SEO scope and fee, advertising-management scope and fee, and media budget as separate items. Buying ad spend alone does not establish eligibility for the SEO + advertising path.
  • Maximum combined-service free period: The maximum website-credit period when buying SEO + advertising, even if the existing website agreement runs longer. No unlimited free period is approved.
  • Start date: When credits begin, when the website and marketing start, and how those dates relate to the existing contract end date.
  • Setup fee: Whether setup is payable, reduced or credited. The recommended standalone website plan’s setup fee is not automatically waived by either proposal.
  • Credit cap and application: The maximum total credit and the CDL website-service invoices it can cover. No cash value, automatic reimbursement or payment to the old provider is offered.
  • Cancellation and credits: The written notice required for month-to-month marketing and the effect of ending SEO, ad management or website service during a credit period. No transition-specific cancellation charge or repayment rule is approved here.
  • Combining offers: Whether a transition credit can be combined with another discount or offer. Combining offers is not automatic.

Credits apply to monthly CDL website-service fees only. They are not cash, a refund or payment of another provider’s charges. You remain responsible for your existing agreement and any cancellation or early-exit fees.

Our recommended standalone website plan remains a separate proposal. Transition credits do not silently replace its setup, payment, scope or handover terms. No ranking, AI recommendation, lead count or sales result is promised.
