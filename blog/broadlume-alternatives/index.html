Broadlume Alternatives for Independent Flooring Stores

Practical guidance from the CDL Marketing team.

By Caleb Foglesong · Published November 18, 2026

An independent flooring store looking at Broadlume alternatives has four real options: another provider that focuses on flooring retailers, a general or local agency working on a website you own, running it in-house on a standard website builder, or staying on changed terms. Which one fits depends on whether you rely on Broadlume's catalog, visualizer and lead tools, or mainly want more local customers.

We are one of the alternatives, so read this knowing that. We have tried to describe the others in their own words and to be plain about where we are not the right fit.

First, list what you actually use

Broadlume, now a Cyncly company, sells a broad flooring technology platform. Cyncly's package table lists product catalogs, sample ordering, visualization, reporting, call tracking and reputation tools, with lead management seats on higher tiers.

Before you compare anyone, write down which of those your store uses every week:

  • The product catalog on your website
  • The room visualizer
  • Sample ordering
  • The lead management tool your salespeople work from
  • Call tracking numbers
  • Review and reputation tools

If several of those are part of your daily routine, you are replacing software as well as marketing, and a platform provider may suit you better than an agency. If you mostly want to be found and to get more calls and visits, your choice is wider.

Option 2: a general or local agency, on a website you own

A capable local agency can run search and advertising for a flooring store. The trade-off is that you will teach them the business: how estimates and measures work, what a good lead sounds like, and which products carry your margin. Ask whether they have a flooring retailer you can call.

Option 3: do it in-house

A store with someone who has a few hours a week can run a standard website builder, a Google Business Profile and a simple review habit without an agency. Our flooring store marketing guide lays out the order to work in. What in-house setups usually lack is a product catalog feed and someone watching the ad account.

Option 4: stay, on better terms

If the tools work for your team and the problem is the price, the term or the service, say so before you leave. A written list of what you want changed is a fair request to make of any provider.

Check these before you give notice

  1. Your agreement. The end date, the renewal terms and the notice you must give.
  2. Your domain name. Who it is registered to, and who can log in.
  3. Your content. Which pages, photos and product information you may take with you.
  4. Your Google Business Profile. That you are an owner on it, in your own account.
  5. Your phone numbers. Whether tracking numbers on your site and ads belong to you.
  6. Your email. Whether store email runs through the same provider.
  7. Redirects. A plan to point old page addresses to the new ones so you do not lose your place in search.

The full list is in our checklist for switching flooring website providers. What we propose for stores moving to us is on the switching page.

Five questions to ask any provider

  1. What exactly will you do each month, in writing?
  2. What will I own if I leave: the website, the domain, the ad accounts, the content?
  3. What is charged separately from the monthly fee?
  4. How much notice do I give to cancel?
  5. How will you tie the work to calls and sold jobs, not only clicks?

FAQ

What are the alternatives to Broadlume for a flooring store?

There are four real options: another provider that focuses on flooring retailers, a general or local agency working on a website you own, running it in-house on a standard website builder, or staying with Broadlume on changed terms. Flooring-focused providers that publish their offers include FloorForce, Floor Launch, Flooring Pros Marketing, Square Foot Media and CDL Marketing.

Is Broadlume the same company as Cyncly?

Broadlume is now a Cyncly company. Its flooring website packages and tools are published on Cyncly's site, and the Broadlume site describes an all-in-one platform for the flooring industry.

Can I keep my website if I leave Broadlume?

That depends on your agreement, so ask in writing before you give notice. Find out who controls your domain name, which pages, photos and product content you may take with you, and whether your phone numbers and Google Business Profile access are in your name.

How do I compare flooring marketing providers fairly?

Ask each one for the same things in writing: what work is included each month, what you will own, what is charged separately, the notice needed to cancel, and how results will be tied to calls and sold jobs. Then compare like for like.
